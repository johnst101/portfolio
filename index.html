About

The longer version.

I grew up outside Milwaukee and moved to Minneapolis in 2017 for the University of Minnesota, where I double-majored in Supply Chain & Operations Management and Management Information Systems. Along the way I interned in project management at Optum, then in supply management at Medtronic, an internship that turned into a full-time offer.

I spent a year and a half building analytics inside Medtronic's supply organization, with a stretch assignment as a Category Manager on the side. A reorganization moved me into a centralized analytics team, where I found I genuinely liked the technical side, two years as a Power BI developer and data engineer, before shifting into product ownership to learn that discipline too.

Now I'm a Principal Advanced Analytics Product Owner by day and a graduate student in Big Data Systems by night, and I'm ready to point both back toward hands-on building. I'm not trying to stay boxed into data, I'm a generalist who goes wide by default and deep when something earns it.

Outside of work: mentoring through my fraternity's alumni board, video games, golf, and hockey.
